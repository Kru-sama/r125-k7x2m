Gift standings

    The track is 2,000 m and the camera follows the leader. 1 gift = 2 m, so 1,000 gifts reach the finish. Every boat also drifts forward slowly at the same speed, but drifting alone never reaches the finish line. Likes and comments do not move boats. Viewers join a team by commenting a country name, and their likes then add effects to their own team's boat. When a team wins, the result shows for 8 seconds and a new round starts. All sounds are generated in the page: tap Sound once to turn them on.
